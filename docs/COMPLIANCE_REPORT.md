## Executive Summary & Simulation vs. Test Reality

This compliance report audits `~/home_monitor_ws` against all requirements defined in **STEP 2 (A to G)** and all scenarios in **STEP 4** of `PROMPT.md`.

### Critical Disclosure: Gazebo Physics vs. Test Suite Simulation

To maintain strict technical honesty, a fundamental distinction is made between what runs in **actual Gazebo Sim Harmonic physics** versus what is **simulated / mocked in automated test harnesses**:

1. **In Live Gazebo Simulation (`ros2 launch smart_home_bringup bringup.launch.py`):**
   - The differential-drive robot (`patrol_robot`), house world (`smart_home_world`), LiDAR (`/scan`), camera (`/camera/image_raw`), odometry (`/odom`), IMU (`/imu`), and `/cmd_vel` truly execute in Gazebo Harmonic physics.
   - The robot physically drives and transitions between rooms (Hallway, Living Room, Dining Room, Entrance, Kitchen).
   - However, without Nav2 global path planning or dynamic obstacle rerouting, navigating tight interior doorways can cause the robot's reactive avoidance to get wedged against doorframes, preventing it from consistently reaching Bedroom 1 in a continuous loop.
   - The intruder scenario in Gazebo spawns a **static** human model in Bedroom 1, **not** an animated walking actor.
   - The obstacle scenario spawns a static box at `(0.0, -1.5, 0.15)` in the hallway.

2. **In Automated Verification Suite (`scripts/verify_all_scenarios.py`):**
   - **Gazebo Sim is NOT running during `verify_all_scenarios.py`**.
   - Instead, individual ROS 2 nodes (`patrol_navigator`, `decision_node`, `detection_node`, `alert_manager_node`) are executed standalone.
   - Sensor inputs (odometry positions, 360° laser scan arrays, OpenCV test images with drawn human shapes, and detection arrays) are **synthetically generated and published over ROS 2 topics** to evaluate node state machines, algorithms, and file logging deterministically.

## 3. Summary of Gaps, Limitations, and Recommendations

1. **Intruder Mobility (Requirement A2 & Scenario 3):**
   - *Current State:* The intruder in `smart_home_gazebo/models/intruder/model.sdf` is a `<static>true</static>` multi-link model placed at a fixed position in Bedroom 1.
   - *Limitation:* It does not walk or move dynamically along waypoints through the house.

2. **Waypoint Configuration Decoupling (Requirement A3 & G2):**
   - *Current State:* The 22 patrol waypoints are hardcoded in C++ in `src/smart_home_navigation/src/patrol_navigator.cpp`.
   - *Limitation:* While zone boundaries and speeds are in `config/zones.yaml` and `config/params.yaml`, editing waypoint coordinates requires recompiling `smart_home_navigation`.

3. **Sudden Fast Motion Detection (Requirement D3 - Rule 5):**
   - *Current State:* Rule 5 evaluates blocked paths when LiDAR front distance is obstructed.
   - *Limitation:* Optical flow or inter-frame image velocity analysis for sudden fast movement is not implemented.

4. **Corridor Navigation Reliability in Live Gazebo Physics (Scenario 1 & 2):**
   - *Current State:* The reactive C++ navigator (`patrol_navigator.cpp`) uses sector-based obstacle avoidance (slow down, stop, turn, backup recovery).
   - *Limitation:* In actual Gazebo physics, traversing narrow doorways (e.g. into Bedroom 1) or navigating around hallway obstacle boxes without global costmap planning (Nav2) can cause the robot to oscillate or stall against doorframes. Full end-to-end multi-room room traversal in tests is validated using simulated odometry topic injection.
